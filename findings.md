# Business Findings Report
## Olist E-Commerce Sales & Logistics Analysis

---

## Key Metrics (From Real Data — 96,470 Orders)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | **BRL 19,880,566** |
| Total Orders Analyzed | **96,470** |
| Average Order Value | **BRL 206.08** |
| Average Delivery Time | **12.0 days** |
| Average Review Score | **4.08 / 5.00** |
| % Late Deliveries | **7.8%** |

---

## Finding 1: Late Deliveries Are the #1 Driver of Dissatisfaction

**Data:**
- On-Time Delivery Avg Review Score: **4.31 / 5.00**
- Late Delivery Avg Review Score: **2.09 / 5.00**
- Score drop for late orders: **51.5%**

**Why it matters:** 7.8% of all orders are late. Every single one of these orders is generating a 2-star review on average — actively damaging the platform's reputation.

**Recommendation:** Add a 2-day buffer to estimated delivery dates for Northeastern and rural states. This converts late deliveries into "on-time" perceptions, lifting scores from 2.1 to 4.3 — without spending a single extra BRL on logistics.

---

## Finding 2: Geographic Revenue Concentration = Business Risk

**Data:**
- São Paulo (SP) alone accounts for ~65% of all orders
- Freight costs to northern states are 3× higher than to SP
- Northern states show significantly lower order volumes despite large populations

**Why it matters:** Over-reliance on one region is a business risk. If any disruption hits SP logistics, revenue collapses.

**Recommendation:** Pilot a regional distribution hub in the Northeast (e.g., Fortaleza or Recife) to cut freight costs and tap into an underserved market of 50M+ consumers.

---

## Finding 3: Office Furniture Is a Silent Bottleneck

**Data:**
- Office Furniture consistently ranks in the bottom 3 categories for review scores
- Higher-than-average delivery delays specifically for bulky item categories
- Health_Beauty and Bed_Bath_Table are top revenue categories with good review scores

**Why it matters:** One underperforming category can disproportionately generate 1-star reviews.

**Recommendation:** Conduct a logistics audit for Office Furniture carriers. Enforce SLA penalties for delay. Consider specialized packaging guidelines for large-format items.

---

## Finding 4: Customer Retention Rate Is Critically Low

**Data (from RFM Analysis):**
- Repeat purchase rate: < 5%
- Most customers buy exactly once and never return
- "Lost" and "At Risk" customer segments represent thousands of customers who haven't purchased in 6+ months

**Why it matters:** Acquiring a new customer costs 5-7x more than retaining an existing one. A 5% improvement in retention can increase revenue by 25-95%.

**Recommendation:** Build an automated post-purchase email series:
- Day 3: "How was your delivery?" (review prompt)
- Day 14: Personalized product recommendations from their purchase category
- Day 30: 10% discount coupon for next purchase

---

## Finding 5: November Causes a Predictable Satisfaction Crisis

**Data:**
- Revenue spikes ~40% in November (Black Friday effect)
- Delivery times also increase ~40% in the same month
- Review scores dip noticeably in November-December

**Why it matters:** The months when Olist makes the most money are also the months with the worst customer experience — creating a loyalty paradox.

**Recommendation:** Pre-hire temporary logistics contractors in October each year. Set delivery expectations to 15+ days during November surge rather than the standard 10-day promise.
